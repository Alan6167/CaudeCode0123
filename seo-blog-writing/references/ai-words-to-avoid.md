# AI-Typical Words to Avoid

This list contains words and phrases that are commonly overused by AI language models and should be avoided in SEO blog writing to maintain natural, human-like content.

## Words to Avoid

**Overused Action Verbs:**
- explore
- discover
- unlock
- delve
- dive into
- spearhead
- foster
- harness
- elevate
- streamline
- leverage

**Overused Descriptive Words:**
- landscape (when used metaphorically, e.g., "business landscape")
- realm
- crucial
- seamless
- robust
- cutting-edge
- game-changer
- groundbreaking
- holistic
- innovative (use sparingly)
- revolutionary (use sparingly)

**Buzzwords:**
- paradigm (especially "paradigm shift")
- synergy
- ecosystem (in business context)
- empower (overused in marketing)
- disruptive
- transformative
- next-generation
- state-of-the-art
- world-class
- leading-edge

## Better Alternatives

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|-------|-------------|
| explore | examine, review, look at, consider |
| discover | find, learn, see |
| unlock | achieve, gain access to, enable |
| delve into | examine closely, investigate, study |
| landscape | market, industry, field, sector |
| crucial | important, essential, necessary |
| seamless | smooth, integrated, easy |
| robust | strong, reliable, effective |
| leverage | use, apply, take advantage of |
| cutting-edge | advanced, latest, modern |
| game-changer | significant development, major improvement |
| streamline | simplify, improve, optimize |
| elevate | improve, enhance, raise |
| ecosystem | network, system, environment |
| empower | enable, help, allow |

## Why Avoid These Words?

1. **Overuse by AI** - These words appear frequently in AI-generated content, making text identifiable as machine-written
2. **Vague Meaning** - Many of these words are abstract and don't provide specific information
3. **Marketing Clichés** - Overused in marketing copy, reducing credibility
4. **Passive Voice** - Some encourage passive construction (e.g., "can be leveraged")
5. **Reader Fatigue** - Audiences are tired of seeing these buzzwords

## Tips for Natural Writing

1. **Be Specific** - Instead of "robust solution," say "reliable software with 99.9% uptime"
2. **Use Active Voice** - "The company provides" instead of "Solutions are provided by the company"
3. **Choose Concrete Words** - "10-year warranty" instead of "world-class guarantee"
4. **Vary Your Vocabulary** - Don't repeat the same descriptive words
5. **Read Aloud** - If it sounds unnatural when spoken, rewrite it

## Context Matters

Some of these words are acceptable in specific contexts:
- **Technical documentation** - "robust" may be appropriate for engineering specs
- **Direct quotes** - If a company uses these words on their website
- **Industry-specific usage** - Some terms have specific meanings in certain fields

When in doubt, choose simpler, more direct language that clearly communicates your message.
